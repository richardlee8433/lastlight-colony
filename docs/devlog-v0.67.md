# Lastlight Colony v0.67 : A Day in the Colony

各位指揮官好！

v0.67 上線了。這次的主題是**生活感**：殖民地有了日夜，有了光影，大家也開始有自己的作息了。

---

## 🌗 日夜循環

殖民地的一天現在大約 5 分鐘：白天比較長，夜晚比較短。

- **黃昏、夜晚、清晨**各有自己的光線顏色。
- **入夜後建築會亮燈**：窗戶透出暖黃色的光，燈條和晶體也會發亮。
- 日夜只是畫面效果，**不影響任何數值**。不喜歡的話，可以在設定頁的「畫面 → 日夜變化」關掉，關掉後永遠是白天。

## ☀️ 光影

- **建築有了影子**：清晨影子又長又斜，中午最短，傍晚再慢慢拉長。
- **小人也有影子**，走進建築的影子裡會跟著變暗。

## 🏠 殖民者的一天

殖民者不再只是來回搬東西了：

- **白天工作**，有時會走進建築裡忙一下再出來。
- **傍晚下班**，到休閒艙吃飯，吃完在門口聊天，頭上會冒出一些小對話泡泡。
- **晚上回生活艙睡覺**，隔天清晨再出門上工。陸戰隊則照常守夜巡邏。

地圖上的小人變成「代表」：人口多了之後，每 10 人才畫一個，畫面更清爽，大家在做什麼也看得更清楚。

## 👣 腳印與街道

- **沙地上會留下腳印**，取代原本的沙土路。
- **石磚路重新規劃**：筆直、寬度一致，像一座真正的城市。
- 殖民者**走在鋪好的路上才會加速**，在沙地上就是一般速度。

## 💨 會動的蒸氣

氧氣再生器和電解站的煙，原本是畫死在圖上的，現在會從煙囪口一團一團冒出來、往上飄散。

## 🔧 修正與調整

- 地圖縮到最小時不會再出現黑邊。
- 開著科技樹時研究會照常進行。
- 第 4 章的目標順序和對話時機調整得更合理。
- 新殖民者是從荒原上找來的倖存者，相關說明從「出生率」改成「倖存者發現速度」。
- 修正高更新率螢幕上，小人走路時左右閃動的問題。

# Lastlight Colony v0.67 : A Day in the Colony

Hello, Commanders!

v0.67 is here, and this one is all about **life in the colony**: day and night, light and shadow, and colonists with routines of their own.

## 🌗 Day and night

A day in the colony now lasts about 5 minutes, with long days and shorter nights.

- **Dusk, night and dawn** each have their own light.
- **Buildings light up after dark**: warm yellow windows, glowing light strips and crystals.
- It's purely visual and **doesn't change any numbers**. If you'd rather not have it, turn off Settings → Display → Day and night cycle, and it stays daytime.

## ☀️ Light and shadow

- **Buildings cast shadows**: long and slanted at dawn, shortest at noon, stretching out again toward evening.
- **Colonists have shadows too**, and they get darker when they walk into a building's shadow.

## 🏠 A colonist's day

Colonists don't just haul goods back and forth anymore:

- **By day they work**, sometimes stepping inside a building for a moment.
- **At dusk they clock off**, eat in the Lounge Pod, then hang around outside chatting, with little speech bubbles popping up.
- **At night they head home** to the Hab Pod and come back out at dawn. Marines keep patrolling through the night.

The colonists you see on the map are now representatives: past 10, there's one for every 10 people, so the map stays clear and it's easier to see what everyone's up to.

## 👣 Footprints and streets

- **Colonists leave footprints in the sand**, replacing the old dirt paths.
- **Stone roads have been replanned**: straight, even and laid out like a real city.
- Colonists **only move faster on paved roads**; on sand they walk at normal speed.

## 💨 Moving steam

The steam on the Oxygen Scrubber and Electrolyzer used to be painted on. Now it puffs out of the chimney and drifts away.

## 🔧 Fixes and tweaks

- No more black borders when you zoom all the way out.
- Research keeps running while the tech tree is open.
- Chapter 4 goals and dialogue timing make more sense now.
- New colonists are survivors found out in the wastes, so "birth rate" is now "survivor discovery".
- Fixed colonists flickering left and right on high-refresh-rate screens.
