# 開發日誌 v0.62：殖民地換新裝，還有了自己的歌

各位指揮官好！

v0.62 上線了。這次的主題很簡單：**讓這顆紅色星球看起來、聽起來都更像一個家。**

---

## 🏜️ 全新場景：從一片沙地開始

以前一開場，地圖上就擺滿了還沒蓋的建築輪廓，像一張寫滿「待辦事項」的便利貼，看了壓力就很大。

現在不一樣了：

- **地面重畫了。**全新手繪的紅土地表，有碎石、有沙紋，風吹起來還有揚塵。
- **只有蓋好的建築才會出現在地圖上。**一開始只有逃生艙和一堆廢料，其他的要靠你一磚一瓦蓋出來。想知道新建築會蓋在哪裡？在下方建造列點一下，地圖上就會出現半透明預覽。
- **道路是走出來的。**新建築蓋好後，才會有一條路連到它。
- **地基會跟著殖民地一起長大。**一開始是踩出來的沙土路。隨著進度推進，靠近中心的道路和地基會先鋪上金屬板，後面再換成石板；外圍剛蓋好的建築還是土路，要升級才會跟上。玩到後期拉遠一看，會看到一圈一圈往外擴張的聚落，很有成就感（我們自己看了也很感動）。

另外，開發途中有人提醒我們：「這顆星球沒有氧氣，路燈裡的火是怎麼燒的？」
……好問題。所以照明全部換成了 LED。科學萬歲。

## 🧑‍🚀 NPC 美術：小人們終於有臉了

殖民者和陸戰隊都換上了全新手繪像素美術：

- **殖民者**：頭盔、太空衣、背包一應俱全，有待機、走路、搬東西、工作四套動作。
- **陸戰隊**：比較壯、比較帥，拿著槍的時候會真的舉槍射擊。

老實說，第一版動畫的小人走路有點像喝醉了：左右左右狂抖，斜著走時還會原地轉圈。我們修了**四輪**：對齊每一格動畫、只在移動時才轉向、位置貼齊像素、加上轉身緩衝。現在他們終於能好好走路了。請大家多多誇獎他們，他們很努力了。

## 🎵 全新背景音樂（這是我們最興奮的部分！）

這次最開心的更新，就是**遊戲有了屬於自己的原創配樂**：

- **首頁主題曲〈Alien Sky〉**：一打開遊戲就聽得到，是這顆星球給你的第一聲招呼。
- **第 1～6 章，每章都有自己的曲子**：從墜落後的孤寂，到殖民地越來越熱鬧，音樂會陪著你一路走下去。換章節時會自然淡入淡出，不會突然切歌。
- **通關之後**，最後一章的音樂會繼續陪著你，慢慢欣賞自己蓋出來的殖民地。

為了不讓大家等太久，音樂是**用到才下載**的：首頁那首會先準備好，其他曲子等你玩到那一章才載入，第一次開遊戲不會卡在讀取畫面。

做音樂的過程很好玩，也很花時間。每一首都是配合那一章的氣氛挑過、調過的。**真心希望你們會喜歡。**如果有哪一首特別打中你，歡迎留言告訴我們！🎧

## 🔧 其他小修正

- 修正第 1 章「緊急營地」在建造列找不到、導致無法推進的問題（抱歉卡到的指揮官們 🙇）。
- 一些版面和效能的小調整。

---

感謝每一位降落在這顆星球上的指揮官。
末光還亮著，我們下次更新見！✨

# Devlog v0.62: A Fresh Coat of Paint, and a Soundtrack of Our Own

Hello, Commanders!

v0.62 is here, and this update has one goal: **make this red planet look and sound more like home.**

## 🏜️ A brand-new world, starting from sand

Before, the map opened already full of outlines for buildings you hadn't built yet. It felt like staring at a very long to-do list. Not anymore:

- **The ground is repainted.** A new hand-painted red terrain with rocks, sand ripples and drifting dust.
- **Only finished buildings appear on the map.** You start with an escape pod and a scrap heap, and everything else is yours to build. Tap a building in the bottom bar to see a translucent preview of where it will go.
- **Roads appear as you build.** Each new building gets its own road once it's finished.
- **The colony paves itself as it grows.** Roads start as dirt tracks. As you progress, the ones near the center upgrade to metal plating and later to stone, while new outer buildings start as dirt until they're upgraded. Zoom out late in the game and you'll see rings of growth spreading outward.

Also, someone pointed out: "There's no oxygen on this planet. How are those lamps burning?" Fair point. Every light is now an LED. Science wins.

## 🧑‍🚀 New NPC art: the colonists finally have faces

Colonists and marines have new hand-drawn pixel art:

- **Colonists** wear helmets, suits and backpacks, with idle, walk, carry and work animations.
- **Marines** are bulkier and cooler, and they actually raise their rifles when they shoot.

Honestly, the first version walked like they'd had one too many: wobbling left and right, even spinning on the spot when walking diagonally. It took **four rounds** of fixes to teach them to walk properly. Please give them some love. They tried really hard.

## 🎵 An all-new soundtrack (the part we're most excited about!)

This is the update we're happiest about: **the game now has its own original music.**

- **Title theme, "Alien Sky"**: the first thing you hear, the planet saying hello.
- **Each of the six chapters has its own track**, from the loneliness after the crash to a busy colony. Tracks crossfade smoothly when a new chapter begins.
- **After the ending**, the final chapter's music keeps playing while you admire what you built.

To keep things fast, music **loads only when it's needed**. The title theme is ready right away, and each chapter's track downloads when you reach that chapter.

Every track was chosen and tuned to fit the mood of its chapter. **We really hope you enjoy it.** If one of them stays with you, let us know in the comments! 🎧

## 🔧 Other fixes

- Fixed the emergency camp not showing in the build bar in chapter 1, which could stop you from progressing (sorry, Commanders 🙇).
- Small layout and performance tweaks.

Thanks to every Commander who has landed on this planet.
The last light is still on. See you next update! ✨
