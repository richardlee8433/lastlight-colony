# Lastlight Colony Release v0.64

各位指揮官好！

v0.64 上線了。這次的重點是**故事**：第 1～4 章的劇情全部重寫，另外還有地圖、道路和暫停功能的改進。

---

## 📖 第 1～4 章劇情重寫

這次把前四章的故事從頭寫了一遍：

- **更多角色的時刻。**大家為什麼會在這顆星球上？每個人心裡放著什麼？這些都會在日常對話裡一點一點透露出來。瑪拉、提歐、朱諾、伊涅絲都多了好幾場戲。
- **殖民地日誌變得更重要了。**有些小故事不會跳出對話框打斷你，而是由朱諾默默寫進日誌。有空的時候翻一翻，你會看到一些只有日誌裡才有的片段。
- **一般殖民者也會開口了。**沒有立繪的殖民者，對話會用旁白樣式呈現。
- **第 4 章的轉折更有份量。**詳細內容就不說了，玩到那裡你就知道 😉

已經玩到後面章節的存檔不受影響：已經過去的劇情不會一次全部跳出來。

## 🛤️ 道路由軌道車線決定

道路的鋪面現在跟**軌道車線**綁在一起：

- 還沒蓋軌道車線：沙土路。
- 蓋好軌道車線：整個殖民地的路一起鋪上**石磚**。
- 升級「雙線運轉」：道路升級成**金屬地磚**。

軌道車線也加進了第 3 章的任務。

## 🗺️ 地圖更細緻

- **地面解析度提高了。**道路邊緣從鋸齒變成平滑的曲線，地磚的接縫也更清楚，跟手繪建築放在一起更搭。
- **拿掉了舊的像素小物件**（路燈、木箱、油桶、碎石），畫面更乾淨。
- **手動採集時建築不會再抖動。**

## ⏸️ 暫停更徹底

暫停時，整個畫面會蓋上一層薄薄的暗幕，所有操作都會停住，不會再發生「時間停了，卻還能採集」的情況。點畫面任何地方，或按 P／空白鍵，就能繼續。

## 🔧 其他調整

- 生活艙和物資庫的面板，改成直接顯示**人口上限**和**倉庫上限**，一眼就知道還住得下多少人、裝得下多少東西。
- 第 2 章的改建技術（電解站、生物採集站）需要先救回伊涅絲才能使用。
- 第 5 章之後的襲擊者調整。
- 「異晶刃」更名為「異晶槍」。

# Lastlight Colony Release v0.64

Hello, Commanders!

v0.64 is here. This update is all about the **story**: chapters 1–4 have been rewritten from the ground up, plus improvements to the map, roads and pausing.

## 📖 Chapters 1–4 story rewrite

We rewrote the first four chapters from the beginning:

- **More character moments.** Why is everyone on this planet? What is each of them carrying? It all comes out little by little in everyday conversation. Mara, Teo, Juno and Ines each have several new scenes.
- **The colony log matters more.** Some small stories don't interrupt you with a dialogue box. Instead, Juno quietly writes them into the log. Take a look when you have a moment. Some moments only live there.
- **Ordinary colonists speak up too.** Colonists without a portrait now talk in a narration-style box.
- **Chapter 4 hits harder.** We won't say more. You'll see when you get there 😉

Saves already further along are safe: past story scenes won't all pop up at once.

## 🛤️ Roads come from the Rail Line

Road paving is now tied to the **Rail Line**:

- No Rail Line yet: dirt paths.
- Build the Rail Line: every road in the colony is paved with **stone tiles**.
- Upgrade to **Double Track**: roads upgrade to **metal tiles**.

The Rail Line is now a chapter 3 goal.

## 🗺️ A sharper map

- **Higher-resolution ground.** Road edges are smooth curves instead of jagged steps, and tile seams are crisper. It all sits much better next to the hand-painted buildings.
- **Old pixel props removed** (lamps, crates, barrels, debris) for a cleaner look.
- **Buildings no longer wobble** when you gather by hand.

## ⏸️ A proper pause

While paused, a dim veil now covers the whole screen and every action stops. No more "time stopped, but I can still gather." Tap anywhere, or press P / Space, to resume.

## 🔧 Other changes

- The Hab Pod and Supply Depot panels now show the **population cap** and **storage cap** directly, so you can see at a glance how many people and how much stock the colony can hold.
- The chapter 2 rebuilds (Electrolyzer, Bio Harvester) now require rescuing Ines first.
- Raiders after chapter 5 have been adjusted.
- Crystal Edge is renamed to Crystal Rifle.
