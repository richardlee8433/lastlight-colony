# 開發日誌 v0.63：殖民地換新裝，還有一顆暫停鍵

各位指揮官好！

v0.63 上線了。這次有兩個主題：**殖民地裡的建築全部重新手繪**，還有**第一則玩家回饋帶來的暫停鍵**。

---

## ⏸️ 暫停鍵來了

我們收到了第一則玩家回饋：希望有暫停鍵，而且跳出訊息視窗時也能自動暫停。好建議，這次就做進去了：

- **右上角多了一顆暫停鍵**（「日誌」旁邊）。鍵盤按 **P** 或 **空白鍵** 也可以。
- **任何視窗開著時，遊戲都會自動暫停**：事件、戰鬥報告、章節開場、科技樹、貿易、設定、日誌，不再只有劇情對話。安心讀完再做決定，殖民地不會趁你看報告的時候偷偷缺氧。
- 暫停時，殖民者和建築動畫也會一起停住，整個世界靜止。但你還是可以移動鏡頭、查看建築、規劃下一步。

謝謝這則回饋！有任何想法，都歡迎繼續留言。

## 🏗️ 第 1～4 章建築，全部重新手繪

上一版我們換了地面和小人，這次輪到建築了。第 1～4 章的 **28 棟建築**全部換成手繪的新圖，每一棟都比以前細緻很多：

- **第 1～2 章：拼裝期。**剛墜落的殖民地，什麼都是用逃生艙殘骸湊出來的：補丁鐵皮、帆布、束線帶、歪歪的天線。緊急營地是三顆充氣圓頂，休閒艙的窗戶裡看得到沙發和盆栽。
- **第 3 章：工程期。**殖民地站穩了腳步，開始用自己的技術蓋規格化的建築：整齊的面板、標準氣閘門、條紋塗裝。鍛造廠的爐窗會發出橘光。
- **第 4 章：換時代了。**建築的樣子和前面完全不同。至於為什麼，就留給你自己去發現吧 😉

慢慢玩下去，地圖上會同時留著每個時期蓋的建築。拉遠看一下，你會看到自己的殖民地一路走過來的歷史。

建築上的窗戶、燈光和機器現在也會在地面上投出柔和的光暈，讓紅色大地上的殖民地更有生氣。

## 🧑‍🚀 殖民者：細節加倍

殖民者和陸戰隊的圖現在用**兩倍的細節**顯示。頭盔反光、臉、背包、太空衣上的條紋都看得清楚了，大小不變，跟新建築站在一起剛剛好。

## 🔜 下一版預告

第 5～6 章的建築還在繪製中。在那之前，玩到後期會看到一些舊款建築混在裡面，請多包涵，下一版就會補齊！

# Devlog v0.63: New Buildings, and a Pause Button

Hello, Commanders!

v0.63 is here. This update is about two things: **every building in the colony has been repainted**, and **a pause button, thanks to our first player feedback**.

## ⏸️ Pause button is here

We got our first player feedback: a pause button, plus auto-pause when message windows pop up. Great idea, so it's in:

- **There's a pause button** in the top-right corner, next to the Log button. You can also press **P** or **Space**.
- **The game auto-pauses whenever any window is open**: events, battle reports, chapter intros, the tech tree, trade, settings and the log, not just story dialogue. Read at your own pace. Your colony won't run out of air while you're reading a report.
- While paused, colonists and building animations freeze too. You can still move the camera, inspect buildings and plan your next move.

Thanks for the feedback! If you have other ideas, keep them coming.

## 🏗️ Chapters 1–4: every building repainted

Last update we repainted the ground and the colonists. This time it's the buildings' turn. All **28 buildings** from chapters 1–4 now have brand-new hand-painted art, far more detailed than before:

- **Chapters 1–2: the salvage era.** A freshly crashed colony where everything is made from escape-pod wreckage: patched metal, tarps, zip ties and crooked antennas. The emergency camp is a cluster of inflatable domes, and you can spot a sofa and potted plants through the lounge windows.
- **Chapter 3: the engineering era.** The colony finds its feet and builds to its own standards: neat panels, proper airlocks and striped paint. The forge's furnace window glows orange.
- **Chapter 4: a new era.** The buildings look nothing like what came before. We'll let you find out why 😉

As you play on, buildings from every era stay on the map. Zoom out and you'll see your colony's history laid out in front of you.

Windows, lamps and machines now cast soft light onto the ground, glowing across the red plains.

## 🧑‍🚀 Colonists: twice the detail

Colonists and marines are now drawn at **twice the detail**. You can see helmet glints, faces, backpacks and suit stripes. They're the same size as before and sit nicely next to the new buildings.

## 🔜 Coming next

The chapter 5–6 buildings are still being painted. Until then you'll see a few old-style buildings late in the game. They'll be replaced in the next update!
