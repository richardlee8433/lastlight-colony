## 🫁 先顧好空氣

以前在這顆星球上，最先撐不住的反而是肚子。這不太對，所以這次把順序調回來了：

- **每位殖民者的耗氧量提高，營養消耗降低。**現在氧氣才是最先見底的資源。
- **逃生艙的維生系統大約撐 5 分鐘。**時間一到，就得靠你自己蓋的氧氣再生器了。
- 開局的庫存也重新分配過，營養比氧氣撐得久。

一句話：**先蓋氧氣，再想晚餐。**

第 1 章原本衝得太快，角色們話還沒說完，就已經進到下一章了。現在第 1 章多了幾個目標：

- 殘骸堆升到 Lv2、氧氣再生器和藻類槽升到 Lv3。
- 第 1 章的建築最高 Lv3。
- **緊急營地**要先完成上面這些才能蓋，材料也比以前多。

多出來的時間，剛好讓大家把話說完。順便拿掉了第 1 章那句「去蓋生活艙」的提示，因為那時候根本還沒有生活艙可以蓋 😅

- **你怎麼經營殖民地，角色們會注意到。**你把資源投在哪裡，會影響之後聽到的對話。
- **結局不只一種。**

細節一樣不說，留給你自己去發現 😉

這是第一版，之後還會繼續補。已經玩到第 5、6 章的舊存檔，會依你之前的做法接上新劇情。

# Lastlight Colony v0.65 : Every Breath Counts & a Story with Choices

Hello, Commanders!

v0.65 is here, with three things: **oxygen** is now the real survival pressure, **chapter 1** takes its time, and **chapters 5–6** have a new story.

## 🫁 Air comes first

On this planet, the first thing to give out used to be everyone's stomach. That didn't feel right, so we flipped it:

- **Each colonist breathes more and eats less.** Oxygen is now the first resource to run dry.
- **The escape pod's life support lasts about 5 minutes.** After that, you're relying on the Oxygen Scrubber you built yourself.
- Starting supplies are rebalanced too, so nutrients last longer than oxygen.

In short: **build oxygen first, worry about dinner later.**

## 🐢 A slower chapter 1

Chapter 1 used to rush by so fast that the crew moved on before they finished talking. It now has a few more goals:

- Scrap Heap to Lv2, Oxygen Scrubber and Algae Tank to Lv3.
- Chapter 1 buildings cap at Lv3.
- The **Emergency Camp** needs those goals done first, and costs a bit more.

That extra time gives everyone room to finish their conversations. We also removed the chapter 1 hint telling you to build Hab Pods, since there aren't any to build yet 😅

## 🧭 Chapters 5–6: your call

The first version of the chapter 5–6 story is in:

- **Your decisions change the story.** Some choices have no right answer, and there's no taking them back.
- **The crew notices how you run the colony.** Where you put your resources shapes the conversations you hear later.
- **There's more than one ending.**

As always, no details. That's for you to find 😉

This is a first pass and we'll keep adding to it. Older saves already in chapter 5 or 6 pick up the new story based on what you did before.
