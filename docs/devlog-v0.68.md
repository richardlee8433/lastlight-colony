# Lastlight Colony v0.68 : First Voices

Hello, Commanders!

v0.68 is here, and this one is all about **sound**: for the first time, the people of the colony are speaking up.

## 🎙️ Voice acting (English)

- **Chapter 1 opening**: Mara narrates the chapter intro herself, and the opening conversation is fully voiced by Mara, Teo and Juno.
- Lines play as they appear and stop when you skip ahead. The music dips a little while someone is talking.
- There's a new **Voice volume** slider in Settings, so you can turn it up, down or off.
- Voices are English only for now, so **they won't play with the Chinese interface** yet.

**We're still working on adding more voice acting!** More lines and more characters are on the way. If you enjoy the voiced version, or have thoughts on how a character sounds, **please let us know!** Your feedback will help us decide which lines to voice next.

## ☀️ Better light and shadow

- Daytime shadows now **lengthen and shorten smoothly** instead of jumping in steps.
- Shadows are easier to see: they show up from dawn to dusk, even under low, wide buildings.

## 🔧 Fixes and tweaks

- Oxygen and nutrients no longer mysteriously run dry after you've been away for a while.
- Once stone roads are paved, colonists stick to them on the way to work, to dinner and back home.
- Colonists chatting outside the Lounge Pod in the evening no longer pile up in one clump at the door.
- Fixed apostrophes in English text showing up extra wide with a stray space (like "we’ re").
- New Privacy option in Settings lets you turn off anonymous play statistics.
