Lastlight Colony v0.70.2 : Upgrades That Matter

A balance update: every building level now makes a real difference, the tech tree is tidier, and the chapter goals are shorter.

---

English

Hello, commanders!

v0.70.2 is a balance update. We played through the game many more times to find details we could improve. If you feel it's gotten better, please be sure to let us know!

🏗️ Every level counts

- The "workers +N" upgrades are gone. In a full run the colony always had more job slots than people to fill them, so these upgrades never did anything.
- Instead, every building level now raises efficiency: each worker produces 5% more per level above Lv1. This applies to gathering, processing and research buildings.
- The building panel shows your current level efficiency, and the upgrade section shows what the next level gives you.
- Old saves: worker-cap upgrades you already bought are removed. Any extra workers simply go back to being idle. Nobody leaves the colony.

👆 Better click upgrades

- The algae tank's crit upgrade is now Select Strain: +20% output. Hardly anyone clicks the algae tank, so a crit bonus there did nothing.
- The scrap heap's On-site Supervisor now raises the click bonus from +25% to +50%. It used to make the bonus last longer, which didn't matter while you held the button.
- The long "hold the collect button" hint in the building panel has been replaced by one line of numbers: click yield, crit chance and multiplier, and click bonus. The first goal of Chapter 1 already teaches you how to collect.

🌙 Safer offline time

- The population no longer grows while you're away. New arrivals used to show up idle, breathing and eating, and early colonies could run out of oxygen in an hour or two. New colonists now arrive once you're back.

🔬 A cleaner tech tree

- 19 research projects in total.
- Precise Handling (+3% crit chance) has been removed: the effect was too small to notice. Reinforced Gloves no longer has a prerequisite.
- Research projects that did the same thing as others have new effects. Crystal Resonance now speeds up research by 25%. Field Medicine (replacing Emergency Rations) helps the injured recover 50% faster.
- Lattice Materials now only needs Warehouse Expansion.
- When something costs more than your storage can hold, the hint now points you to the storage research as well as the Supply Depot.

🎯 Shorter chapter goals

- Chapter goals went from 39 to 28. Side errands that had little to do with the story are gone, and goals that overlapped have been merged.
- Goals are there to guide you, not to give you homework. Your progress on the remaining goals carries over.
- A full playthrough now takes about 75 minutes, down from about 85.

🔧 Your saves carry over. Existing colonies are converted automatically.

Thank you for playing and for all your feedback! Tell us how the new upgrades feel.
The last light is still burning. See you in the next update! ✨

繁體中文

各位指揮官好！

v0.70.2 是一次平衡更新。我們又玩了很多次，想知道哪些細節可以優化。如果你覺得有進步，請一定要告訴我們！

🏗️ 每一級都有感

- 拿掉了「工人上限 +N」的升級。整場玩下來，殖民地的工位一直比人多，這些升級其實從來用不到。
- 改成每升一級就提高效率：建築每比 Lv1 高一級，每位工人的產量就多 5%。採集、加工和研究建築都適用。
- 建築面板會顯示目前的等級效率，升級區塊也會寫出下一級的效果。
- 舊存檔：已經買過的工人上限升級會移除，多出來的工人回到閒置，不會有人離開殖民地。

👆 更實用的點擊升級

- 藻類槽的暴擊升級改成「精選藻種」：產量 +20%。藻類槽幾乎沒有人在點，暴擊加成等於沒有。
- 殘骸堆的「現場督導」改成把點擊加成從 +25% 提高到 +50%。原本是讓加成持續更久，但按住按鈕時加成本來就不會斷。
- 建築面板裡那段「按住採集按鈕……」的長說明，改成一行數字：點擊量、暴擊率與倍率、點擊加成。怎麼採集，第 1 章第一個目標就教過了。

🌙 離線更安心

- 離線時人口不再增加。以前新來的殖民者會在你不在時抵達，只閒著耗氧氣、吃營養，前期殖民地離線一兩個小時，氧氣就可能見底。現在新殖民者會等你回來才抵達。

🔬 更清楚的科技樹

- 總共 19 項研究。
- 拿掉「精準操作」（暴擊率 +3%，效果小到感覺不出來）；強化手套不再需要前置研究。
- 效果跟別項重複的研究換了新效果：異晶共振改成研究速度 +25%；「戰地醫療」取代原本的「應急口糧」，讓傷員恢復快 50%。
- 晶格建材現在只需要倉儲擴建。
- 東西的價格超過倉儲上限時，提示除了物資庫，也會告訴你可以研究標準化貨架或倉儲擴建。

🎯 更精簡的章節目標

- 章節目標從 39 項減為 28 項：拿掉跟主線關係不大的支線，重複的目標也合併了。
- 目標是用來引導，不是讓你寫作業。保留下來的目標，進度都會沿用。
- 整場遊戲大約 75 分鐘可以玩完，以前約 85 分鐘。

🔧 舊存檔可以繼續玩，殖民地會自動轉換。

謝謝大家的遊玩和回饋！新的升級玩起來感覺如何，都歡迎告訴我們。
末光還亮著，我們下次更新見！✨
