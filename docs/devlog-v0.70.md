Lastlight Colony v0.70 : Trade and the Shipyard

Our biggest update yet: new faces, a merchant in orbit, and a ship of your own.

---

English

Hello, commanders!

v0.70 is here, and it is the biggest update since the colony first landed. Here is what's new.

🎭 Characters with more feeling

- All seven main characters now have expression portraits. During conversations their faces change line by line: angry, sad, or laughing.
- The Chapter 1 opening was re-recorded with more emotion, and every chapter introduction (Chapters 1 to 6) is now narrated by Mara.

🛒 A merchant in orbit (Chapter 4)

- An independent trader called the Magpie makes contact in Chapter 4. Build a Trading Post to start doing business with him.
- Sell metal, tools, parts and nutrient. Buy four goods the colony can't make on its own: electronics, rare metals, fuel and medicine.
- Prices follow the merchant's stock. Sell a lot and he pays less; buy a lot and he charges more. Arrows show where prices are heading.
- Orders arrive by container: it parachutes down next to the Trading Post a minute later and goes into the cargo hold.
- New Chapter 4 goal: receive your first container.

🚀 The Shipyard (Chapters 3 to 6)

- From Chapter 3 you can build a Shipyard and assemble a ship, one module at a time: six modules in all.
- Later modules need imported goods, so trading and shipbuilding go hand in hand.
- Every finished module brings a short scene, and the shipyard changes as the ship takes shape.
- The ship matters in Chapter 6. The big decision now has a new option, and what you do with the ship is up to you.

📈 Chapter 5 trade

- Who you trade with depends on the choices you make in Chapter 5.
- Market events can shake up prices for a while: disrupted routes, tighter blockades, and surprise supply runs.
- The Med Bay can now use medicine to help the injured recover faster.
- The scrap-for-xenocrystal "Mysterious Signal" trade has been retired to keep trading focused.

🔬 Simpler research

- The Tech Institute and the Xenology Institute are now one building. In Chapter 5 you rebuild the Tech Institute into the Xenology Institute. Your researchers and progress carry over.

🎨 New icons

- All 14 resources have new hand-painted icons: in the top bar, panels, the market and the collect buttons on the map.

🔧 Your saves carry over. Existing colonies are converted automatically.

Thank you for playing and for all your feedback! Let us know what you think of the Magpie and the shipyard.
The last light is still burning. See you in the next update! ✨

繁體中文

各位指揮官好！

v0.70 上線了，這是殖民地降落以來最大的一次更新。以下是這次的新內容。

🎭 更有表情的角色

- 七位主要角色都有了表情立繪。對話時，表情會一句一句跟著變：生氣、難過、大笑。
- 第 1 章開場重新錄音，情緒更飽滿；第 1～6 章的章節開場說明，現在都由瑪拉旁白。

🛒 軌道上的商人（第 4 章）

- 第 4 章，一位叫「喜鵲」的獨立行商會跟殖民地聯絡。蓋好交易站，就能跟他做生意。
- 賣出金屬、工具、零件和營養；買進殖民地自己造不出來的四種貨：電子元件、稀有金屬、燃料、醫療物資。
- 價格跟著行商的庫存走：賣得多，他收得便宜；買得多，他賣得貴。箭頭會顯示價格的走向。
- 下單後大約一分鐘，貨櫃會掛著降落傘落在交易站旁邊，放進貨艙。
- 第 4 章新目標：等到第一個貨櫃落地。

🚀 船塢（第 3～6 章）

- 從第 3 章起可以蓋船塢，一個模組一個模組地把船造起來，總共六個模組。
- 後面的模組要用到進口品，所以交易和造船是連在一起的。
- 每完成一個模組都有一段小對話，船塢的樣子也會跟著船的進度改變。
- 第 6 章，這艘船會派上用場。重大抉擇多了一個新選項，要怎麼用這艘船，由你決定。

📈 第 5 章的交易

- 你在第 5 章做的選擇，會決定之後跟誰交易。
- 市場事件會讓價格起伏一陣子：航線中斷、封鎖收緊，還有意外的補給。
- 醫療艙現在可以用醫療物資，讓傷員恢復得更快。
- 原本用廢料換異晶的「神秘訊號」交易已經拿掉，讓交易更單純。

🔬 研究更簡單

- 科技研究院和異星研究院合併成一棟：第 5 章把科技研究院改建成異星研究院，研究員和研究進度都會保留。

🎨 新圖示

- 14 種資源全部換成新的手繪圖示：頂部資源列、各種面板、市場，以及地圖上的採集按鈕。

🔧 舊存檔可以繼續玩，殖民地會自動轉換。

謝謝大家的遊玩和回饋！喜鵲和船塢玩起來感覺如何，都歡迎告訴我們。
末光還亮著，我們下次更新見！✨
