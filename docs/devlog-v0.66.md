# Lastlight Colony v0.66 : A Skyline Worth Defending

各位指揮官好！

v0.66 上線了。這次是一個大改版：**每一棟建築都換上了手繪版本**。從墜落時的逃生艙，到最後的軌道信標，整座殖民地終於全部畫完了。

---

## 🏙️ 第 5～6 章：殖民地長成一座城市

第 5～6 章的 10 棟建築全部重畫，殖民地從一個求生營地，正式長成一座有天際線的城市：

- **星城穹頂**現在是地圖正中央的地標，透明穹頂下看得到整片星圖。
- **行政中心、總督府**有了藍色旗幟、廣場和噴泉，看起來像大家真的會在這裡開會的地方。
- **天幕住宅**一層層的住宅艙裡亮著暖黃色的窗，陽台上還掛著曬衣繩。
- **太空港**停著一艘真正的船。
- **防禦砲塔、異星研究院**換上了更有科技感的外型。

還有幾棟建築看起來……不太像人類蓋的。至於為什麼，就留給你自己去想 😉

## 🪖 突擊隊來了！

企業突擊隊終於有了自己的樣子。以前他們只是借用異星生物的身體、換個顏色就上場，現在是一整隊全副武裝的士兵：

- **會舉槍還擊。**突擊隊會轉向你的陸戰隊，點放曳光彈，槍口還會冒火光。
- **中彈有反應。**被子彈或砲塔雷射打中，會往後一縮。
- **擊退後會倒下。**他們會跪下、撲倒，在地上躺一會兒才消失。

米白裝甲、紅色標誌，跟你那群穿深藍色、戴圓頭盔的陸戰隊一眼就分得出來。

# Lastlight Colony v0.66 : A Skyline Worth Defending

Hello, Commanders!

v0.66 is here, and it's a big one: **every building in the game is now hand-painted**. From the escape pod you crashed in to the final Orbital Beacon, the whole colony is finally drawn.

## 🏙️ Chapters 5–6: the colony becomes a city

All 10 buildings in chapters 5–6 have been redrawn, and the colony has grown from a survival camp into a city with a real skyline:

- **The Star Dome** is now the landmark at the center of the map, with a whole star chart under its glass.
- **The Administration Center and Governor's Hall** have blue banners, a plaza and fountains. They look like places where people actually meet.
- **The Sky Residence** glows with warm windows on every tier, with laundry lines strung across the balconies.
- **The Spaceport** has a real ship parked on the pad.
- **The Defense Turret and Xeno Research Institute** got a much more high-tech look.

A few of the buildings look... not quite human-made. Why? We'll let you think about that 😉

## 🪖 The commandos are here!

Corporate commandos finally look like themselves. They used to borrow the aliens' bodies with a new coat of paint. Now they're a squad of fully armed soldiers:

- **They shoot back.** Commandos turn to face your marines and fire bursts of tracer rounds, muzzle flash and all.
- **They react to hits.** A bullet or a turret laser knocks them back a step.
- **They go down when you win.** They drop to a knee, fall flat and lie there for a moment before they're gone.

Off-white armor with red insignia, easy to tell apart from your marines in their dark blue suits and round helmets.

## 🔧 Other fixes

- Fixed the ending screen sometimes appearing before the last conversation.
- Fixed characters on some routes mentioning things you haven't researched yet.
