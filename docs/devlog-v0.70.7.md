Lastlight Colony v0.70.7 : Smoother Start, Better on Phones

A friendlier first few minutes, a cleaner layout on phones and small screens, a hand-painted cargo drop from the Magpie, and a few fixes.

---

English

Hello, commanders!

Thanks to everyone who tried the game since the last update. Your play data showed us that many players left in the first minute, especially on phones, so this update is mostly about making the start easier to get into. If you feel it's gotten better, please be sure to let us know!

📱 Better on phones

- The colony panel folds into a small bar showing population, idle colonists and the most urgent warning. It flashes red when oxygen or food runs low. Tap it to open the full panel.
- Only one of the quest list and the colony panel is open at a time, so they no longer cover each other or the map.
- The building panel takes up only the lower half of the screen, with smaller icons. Longer descriptions are tucked behind "More".
- The Chapter 1 intro shows just the first paragraph (tap "More" for the rest). Instead of written instructions, a bouncing finger points at the scrap heap's collect button until you collect for the first time.
- The first conversation is shorter.

🚀 A smoother start

- On CrazyGames, pressing Play takes you straight into the game. If you have a save, you pick up where you left off. You can change the language in Settings.
- Chapter 1 is about three survivors, and now it really is: nobody else arrives in Chapter 1. Survivors from the other pods find the colony in Chapter 2. The colony panel shows just "3" in Chapter 1 instead of "3/4", so it no longer looks like someone is missing.
- On short screens (such as the CrazyGames window on a laptop), the colony panel folds away while the quest list is open, so they don't overlap.

📦 The Magpie's cargo drop

- The cargo the Magpie drops next to the Trading Post is now hand-painted: a weathered teal-and-white container with orange bumpers and a magpie stencil, under an orange-and-white patched parachute.
- The parachute sways on the way down, then tips over and collapses when the container lands.

🔧 Fixes

- Fixed text that sometimes showed up garbled (like "���") on some sites.
- From Chapter 4 there are ten resources, and the resource bar at the top could run off the screen. It now stops before the buttons in the top-right corner and scrolls sideways: drag it on a phone, or use the mouse wheel on a computer.
- Old saves keep working.

Thanks for playing and for all your feedback! Tell us how the new start feels.
The last light is still on. See you in the next update! ✨

繁體中文

各位指揮官好！

謝謝上次更新後來玩的每一位。遊玩數據告訴我們，很多玩家在第一分鐘就離開了，手機上尤其明顯，所以這次更新主要是讓開頭更容易上手。如果你覺得變好了，一定要告訴我們！

📱 手機更好玩

- 殖民地面板收成一條小膠囊，顯示人口、閒置人數和最急的警告；缺氧或缺糧時會閃紅框。點一下就展開完整面板。
- 任務清單和殖民地面板一次只展開一個，不會再互相疊住或蓋住地圖。
- 建築面板只佔畫面下半部，圖示也縮小了；比較長的說明收進「看更多」。
- 第 1 章開場說明只先顯示第一段（其餘按「看更多」）。操作說明拿掉了，改成一根上下擺動的手指，指著殘骸堆的採集按鈕，第一次採集後就消失。
- 第一段對話變短了。

🚀 開頭更順

- 在 CrazyGames 按 Play 就直接進遊戲；有存檔就接著玩。語言可以在設定裡切換。
- 第 1 章的故事是三個倖存者，現在真的只有三個人：第 1 章不會有新殖民者抵達，其他逃生艙的人第 2 章才會找到殖民地。第 1 章的殖民地面板只顯示「3」，不再顯示「3/4」，看起來不會像少了一個人。
- 矮的畫面（例如筆電上的 CrazyGames 遊戲框）：任務清單展開時，殖民地面板會暫時收起來，兩個不會疊在一起。

📦 喜鵲號的空投

- 喜鵲號投到交易站旁的貨櫃換成手繪圖：用舊的藍綠白鐵箱、橘色防撞角、正面有鵲鳥剪影，掛在橘白相間、打過補丁的降落傘下。
- 降落傘落下時會輕輕擺動，貨櫃落地後降落傘會倒向一邊、塌下來。

🔧 修正

- 修正在部分平台上，文字偶爾變成亂碼（像「���」）的問題。
- 第 4 章起有 10 種資源，上方的資源列會超出畫面。現在資源列會停在右上角按鈕前面，放不下的可以橫向捲動：手機用手指滑，電腦用滑鼠滾輪。
- 舊存檔可以繼續玩。

謝謝大家的遊玩和回饋！新的開頭玩起來感覺如何，都歡迎告訴我們。
末光還亮著，我們下次更新見！✨
